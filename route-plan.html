Key Cost-Saving Actions
Do not remobilize — finish Southeast first
The current field team is already in GA/FL. Redirecting them now and returning later for the remaining 330 SE stores would cost $40–60K in redeployment (flights, hotel, freight restaging). Complete all 10 SE states through approximately Week 15 before moving north.
Northeast corridor: highest-efficiency phase in the program
NY/NJ/PA alone = 240 stores within 150 miles of Newark — 60-store weeks are achievable here, vs. 50 everywhere else. Single staging warehouse at EWR covers the entire cluster. This is the one phase where adding a second team simultaneously has a strong ROI case.
California is a program within the program — treat it that way
235 CA stores = 12% of the total remaining program. Lease a staging warehouse in Ontario, CA (covers LA basin + Inland Empire). Plan 4 pure CA weeks before rotating north. Do not mix CA with OR/WA — the geography penalty eliminates the efficiency gain of clustering.
Start Canada authorization at Week 16 — not later
ATA carnet processing, Cisco Canada work authorizations, and cross-border equipment import typically take 4–6 weeks. Miss this window and Canada slips off the parallel track and onto the critical path, adding 3–4 weeks to the total program timeline. Assign an owner to this action this week.
Consider a second TX-only sub-team to compress Phase 4
TX has 186 stores — at 50/week that's 4 sequential weeks. With two parallel sub-teams (DFW-north and Houston-south), TX could complete in 2 weeks. The incremental mobilization cost is roughly one set of flights + hotel (~$8K) against saving 2 full weeks of program timeline.
Bundle territory logistics into one coordinated trip
PR (10) and USVI (2) share an Atlantic air mobilization — plan them together out of SJU. Guam (2) requires Pacific routing; schedule this to overlap with the West Coast team's AK trip to share freight shipping costs. Pre-ship all island equipment 3 weeks in advance — no same-week freight to island territories.
